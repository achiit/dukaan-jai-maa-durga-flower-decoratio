Meet the owner

Yash Kumar

I started this work about five years ago in our village, Babhandih, with a few baskets of marigold and a lot of learning from relatives who decorated for family weddings. The first mandap I made for a neighbour is still a memory I smile at.

Today people call us from Barwadih and around Latehar for weddings, ring ceremonies, birthdays and even car decoration. Whatever the function, I treat it like it is my own family's.

Tell me your theme and budget on WhatsApp. I will suggest what fits, and I will not push you to spend more.
